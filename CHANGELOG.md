# Changelog

## [0.0.3]

### Fixed
- Restore auto-fill behavior after saving a trip as a draft. (#27)

## [0.0.2]

### Fixed
- Draft trips not visible in the trips menu. (#25)

## [0.0.1]

### Added
- Trip drafts, private/business trip types, and trip details including dates, postal codes, odometer readings, license plate, and notes.
- Auto-fill defaults from the last completed trip, with an option to pre-fill a return trip.
- Yearly and date-range statistics for mileage and travel time, split by private and business use.
- Theme, layout, and auto-fill settings.
- Encrypted local trip storage using SQLCipher and Android Keystore-backed key management.
